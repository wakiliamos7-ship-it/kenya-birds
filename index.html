Birds of Kenya

About Me

Hi, I'm Amos Wakili Mutemi, a web development student from Kenya. I put this page together to show off some of the most beautiful birds in my country, from the Lilac-breasted Roller to the Lesser Flamingo. Kenya is one of the best places in the world for birdwatching, and I wanted to share a few of my favorites.

My Favorite Birds

Lilac-breasted Roller

Kenya's national bird The Lilac-breasted Roller is a colorful bird found in sub-Saharan Africa. It is known for its vibrant plumage and acrobatic flight displays.

Photo credit: Ginger Hill

Grey Crowned Crane

Known for the golden crown of stiff feathers on its head. It lives in wetlands and grasslands and is famous for its dancing displays.

Photo: Susan Gibbons, via [source]

Lesser Flamingo

Gathers by the hundreds of thousands on Kenya's soda lakes, such as Lake Nakuru and Lake Bogoria. Its pink color comes from the algae it filters out of the water.

Photo: Beau Bellman, via [source]

African Fish Eagle

Easy to spot by its white head and chest. It hunts fish from lakeside perches, and its loud, ringing call is one of the classic sounds of Kenya's lakes.

Photo: Elis Cora, via [source]

Secretary Bird

A tall bird of prey that hunts mostly on foot, striding across the savanna. It stamps on snakes and other prey with its long, strong legs.

Photo: Kristen Muir, via [source]
