Birds of Kenya

About Me

Hi, I'm Amos Wakili Mutemi, a web development student from Kenya. I put this page together to show off some of the most beautiful birds in my country, from the Lilac-breasted Roller to the Lesser Flamingo. Kenya is one of the best places in the world for birdwatching, and I wanted to share a few of my favorites.

My Favorite Birds

Lilac-breasted Roller

Kenya's national bird The Lilac-breasted Roller is a colorful bird found in sub-Saharan Africa. It is known for its vibrant plumage and acrobatic flight displays.
